PlayWit Creations - creature models

The models in this pack are other people's openly licensed work, changed for our server.
The changed models (.bbmodel source) are published next to this pack.

  license: licenses/Starcatcher21__Stargazer-Minecraft-Mod.txt

Ember Wisp (qm_wisp)
  from:    WindElemental by Nexusrealms, creature-mod @2b57df2 (https://github.com/Nexusrealms/creature-mod), MIT
  changes: frame recoloured from blue to ember orange/red, all parts glow, idle bob added
  license: licenses/Nexusrealms__creature-mod.txt

Abyss Jelly (qm_jelly)
  from:    Jellyfish from TerraFirmaCraft @54642af (https://github.com/TerraFirmaCraft/TerraFirmaCraft), EUPL-1.2

  license: licenses/TerraFirmaCraft__TerraFirmaCraft.txt
